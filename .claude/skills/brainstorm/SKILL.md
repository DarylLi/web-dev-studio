# /brainstorm — Explore Product Ideas

**Purpose:** Explore the problem space and rough out a few product concepts before committing to one.

## Inputs
- A rough idea, target user, or problem statement

## Steps
1. Ask what problem this solves and for whom
2. Propose 2-4 concept directions with pros/cons
3. Let the user pick a direction or blend ideas

## Outputs
- A short concept brief to feed into /prd

## Agents Invoked
- product-lead
- business-analyst
- ux-researcher
