# /roadmap — Build/Update the Roadmap

**Purpose:** Lay out what ships in which timeframe, tied to the prioritized backlog and capacity.

## Inputs
- Prioritized backlog, team capacity

## Steps
1. Group prioritized items into now/next/later
2. Flag dependencies and risks
3. Get product-director approval

## Outputs
- docs/roadmap.md

## Agents Invoked
- product-lead
- product-director
- delivery-manager
