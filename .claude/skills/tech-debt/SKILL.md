# /tech-debt — Log/Review Tech Debt

**Purpose:** Track shortcuts taken under deadline pressure so they get revisited deliberately, not forgotten.

## Inputs
- A PR or sprint retrospective

## Steps
1. Log the shortcut and its risk
2. Estimate cost to fix later
3. Add to the tech-debt backlog with priority

## Outputs
- An entry in docs/tech-debt.md

## Agents Invoked
- tech-director
- backend-lead
- frontend-lead
